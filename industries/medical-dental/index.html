THE CHALLENGE

Why SEO in this vertical is different.

Before we tell you what works, here’s what makes this industry’s SEO uniquely difficult — and why generic agencies fail at it.

⚠️
YMYL content quality bar is brutal
Medical content is held to Your Money or Your Life quality standards — meaning Google evaluates E-E-A-T more strictly than in any other vertical. Shallow or inaccurate content gets demoted hard. We write to medical review standards.
🏥
Multi-location = compounding complexity
One dental practice is manageable. Twelve locations across three states means 12 GBPs, 12 sets of local pages, 12 review streams, and 12 chances to trigger duplicate content penalties if you're not careful.
🔒
HIPAA makes review responses a minefield
You can't confirm whether someone was a patient, can't discuss treatment details, and can't share specifics even in positive reviews. Every review response has to be crafted to comply — and most agencies have no idea.

DEEPER CONTEXT

Why healthcare SEO has to be built from the ground up for your vertical.

YMYL sets a higher bar for every page

Medical and dental content falls under Google's YMYL (Your Money or Your Life) classification, meaning the quality guidelines are stricter. Treatment content needs medical accuracy. Provider profiles need credentials. Reviews need authenticity signals. Shortcuts that work in e-commerce or B2B SEO fail immediately in healthcare — we don't take them.

Per-location GBPs are the patient-acquisition engine

For most practices, Google Business Profile is the single highest-ROI marketing asset. Patients search 'dentist near me' or 'pediatrician [city]' and choose from the 3-pack. Each location needs its own optimized GBP with active reviews, fresh photos, weekly posts, and HIPAA-compliant responses. Multi-location practices with 10+ GBPs need systems, not one-off management.

Procedure content is the long-tail goldmine

Patients researching a procedure read 8-12 content pieces before booking. If your site isn't in those 8-12, you're not in their consideration set. Procedure-specific pages (Invisalign, dental implants, Botox, LASIK, knee replacement) written with clinical depth and proper schema rank for hundreds of long-tail queries per page.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions specific to this industry.

What business owners and marketing leads in this vertical most often ask us.

How do you handle HIPAA compliance in review responses?

Every response we write follows a strict HIPAA-compliant framework: we never confirm whether someone was a patient, never reference treatment details, never share specifics. Positive reviews get warm, generic thank-yous. Negative reviews get professional acknowledgment and an invitation to take the conversation offline. Every response is reviewed against a compliance checklist before posting.

Do you write medical content yourselves, or does it need a clinician?

We write content that’s medically accurate and citation-backed, but for anything involving specific clinical claims, treatment efficacy, or procedure risks we recommend (and many of our clients require) clinician review before publishing. We work directly with your in-house clinical team or can connect you with medical writers when needed. E-E-A-T benefits significantly from visible medical reviewers.

How do you handle multi-location duplicate content?

Every location page we build has genuinely unique content — not just a swapped city name. Each page includes location-specific context (neighborhoods served, practice hours, provider bios for that site, city-filtered testimonials, insurance accepted at that location). We’ve deployed 120-page rollouts without a single duplicate content flag. Our internal similarity threshold is well under Google’s penalty trigger.

What's the right cadence for reviews per location?

For most medical practices, 10-30 new reviews per month per location is the target range. Dental practices sit higher (more patient touchpoints per month), specialty practices lower. Our review acquisition automation scales to your patient volume — every visit triggers a compliant request, with reasonable frequency caps. Results: our healthcare clients typically 3-5× their prior review velocity within 60 days.

Are you familiar with healthcare-specific directories?

Yes — Healthgrades, Zocdoc, Vitals, WebMD, Yelp, RateMDs, and specialty directories (like the AAD for dermatologists or ADA for dentists). Consistency across these platforms is part of NAP management. We also manage insurance directory listings, which most agencies skip and which drive meaningful new-patient volume.

Can you help with accessibility compliance for ADA-sensitive practices?

Yes. Every Rank-Ready website ships with WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, which is the standard regulators and plaintiffs’ attorneys evaluate against. We also run accessibility audits as a standalone service for existing sites. This isn’t just legal protection — accessibility signals correlate with SEO rankings and expand your addressable patient base.

How long before new patient volume shifts?

Our healthcare clients typically see first ranking movement in 30-60 days (GMB and location pages indexing), meaningful appointment volume lift by month 3-4, and fully compounded growth by month 6-9. Local-focused work (GBP, location pages) moves faster than semantic content depth; we sequence accordingly so you see early wins.
