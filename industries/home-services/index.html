DEEPER CONTEXT

Why home services SEO is 80% local, 20% everything else.

The 3-pack is the whole game

For home services, the Google Maps 3-pack captures 60-70% of all local search clicks. Organic results below the 3-pack pick up the remainder, but the 3-pack is where the phone rings. Which means your Google Business Profile isn't marketing — it's infrastructure. A well-managed GBP with active reviews and high prominence will outperform a beautiful website without those signals every time.

Service-area pages vs. location pages

If you serve 15 cities from one central office, you don't need 15 storefronts — you need 15 service-area pages, each using proper Service schema with areaServed targeting, unique local content, and internal linking that signals geographic hierarchy. This is technically different from multi-location business pages, and handling it wrong triggers 'hidden location' penalties.

Reviews are the difference between growth and survival

Among otherwise-similar local businesses, review velocity and count are the biggest differentiators in local rankings. A plumber with 400 reviews at 4.8 stars outranks a better plumber with 40 reviews at 5.0. Systematic review acquisition after every service call isn't optional — it's the core operational SEO task. We automate it so it happens without your dispatchers having to remember.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions specific to this industry.

What business owners and marketing leads in this vertical most often ask us.

How fast can we move up in the 3-pack?

If you have a moderately populated GBP and are currently outside the 3-pack, expect first movement in 30-45 days and consistent 3-pack presence by 60-90 days. If you’re already in the 3-pack but ranked #3, we can typically move you to #1 in similar timeframes. If you’re starting from a nearly-empty profile, add 30 days for foundational setup and review velocity to compound.

We serve 15+ cities from one office. Can we rank in all of them?

Yes — with the right setup. Service-area pages (distinct from location pages) use Service schema with areaServed targeting, genuinely unique content per city, and internal linking structures that signal geographic hierarchy. We’ve helped home services companies rank in 20+ cities from a single physical location. The key is avoiding ‘hidden location’ signals that trigger Google’s local spam filters.

What about storm response — can SEO handle sudden demand spikes?

For roofing, tree services, water restoration, and similar storm-responsive trades, we build dormant storm-response content that activates during events. Storm damage guides, insurance claim walkthroughs, emergency landing pages — all published months ahead, ranked, and ready to capture the search spike when a storm actually hits. Reactive SEO can’t compete; banked content can.

How do we get more Google reviews?

Our review acquisition automation triggers a request SMS/email after every completed job, sent to the customer’s phone or email within 30 minutes of service completion. Direct link to your Google review URL. Our home services clients typically hit 8-20 new reviews/month/location after 30-60 days. 100% compliant with Google’s review policies — no gating, no incentives.

Should we focus on Google Ads or SEO?

Both, with different roles. Google Ads for immediate emergency-service CPC capture — especially valuable when SEO is still compounding. SEO for durable, ever-compounding organic presence. Most of our home services clients run both. We don’t manage ads (that’s a different specialty), but we coordinate with your PPC vendor so the two strategies reinforce each other.

How do we handle seasonal demand in SEO?

Build the authority before the season. HVAC companies should rank for ‘AC repair’ in March, not July. Roofing companies need storm-season content published and indexed 3-6 months ahead of peak hurricane or hail season. We plan content calendars around your seasonal cycle so ranking power is banked when demand arrives.

What about franchise locations — do they compete with each other?

Franchise SEO requires careful territory management to avoid cannibalization. We architect franchise SEO programs so each location targets distinct city/ZIP territories, shares brand authority without stealing each other’s rankings, and uses proper schema to signal boundaries. We’ve worked with franchise networks from 10 to 100+ locations.
