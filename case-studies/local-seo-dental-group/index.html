01

Client overview

MetroDental Group is a multi-state dental service organization with 120 locations. Patients search locally — “dentist near me”, “Invisalign in [city]” — and choose from whoever ranks at the top. Each location needed its own visibility in its own market.

02

The challenge

The group was fighting for visibility in only a couple of cities. Scaling to 120 markets brought the classic multi-location problems:

  • Every location needed its own rankable page — but “city-swap” templates trigger duplicate content problems.
  • Healthcare is a YMYL category, so thin or inaccurate content gets demoted hard.
  • 120 locations means 120 Google Business Profiles, 120 review streams and 120 chances to get it wrong.
03

Our approach

  1. 1

    Local-intent research per market

    We mapped how patients search in each city — treatments, insurance questions and neighborhood terms — so every page targets what that market actually looks for.

  2. 2

    Semantic page templates with genuinely unique content

    Each location page was built on a semantic template but filled with location-specific substance, not a swapped city name.

    • Neighborhoods served, practice hours and directions
    • Provider bios for that site and the services offered there
    • City-filtered testimonials and insurance accepted at that location
  3. 3

    Full local schema on every page

    LocalBusiness and Service schema on each page, with NAP details matching the location’s Google Business Profile exactly.

  4. 4

    Similarity control at scale

    Every page was checked against an internal similarity threshold kept well under Google’s penalty trigger — which is how 120 pages shipped with zero duplicate content flags.
