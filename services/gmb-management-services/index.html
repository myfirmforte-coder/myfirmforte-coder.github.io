WHY GBP MANAGEMENT MATTERS

The 3-pack captures 44% of all local search clicks. Are you in it?

Proximity, prominence, and relevance — the three pillars

Google's local ranking algorithm evaluates three core signals: proximity (how close you are to the searcher), prominence (how well-known and trusted your business is, driven largely by reviews and citations), and relevance (how accurately your profile matches what they're searching for). You can't change proximity, but the other two are entirely controllable — which is why professionally managed GBPs consistently outrank do-it-yourself profiles.

Reviews are the single biggest prominence signal

Review count, velocity, recency, sentiment, and keyword density all factor into local rankings. Businesses that actively solicit reviews from every customer — and respond to every single one — consistently outperform competitors with higher star averages but lower volume. Review acquisition is a system, not a hope.

Posts, photos, and Q&A signal active management

Google favors businesses that treat their GBP as a living asset. Weekly posts signal that the business is open and operating. Regular photo uploads signal authenticity and freshness. Active Q&A signals engagement. These signals compound — a profile with all three consistently outranks one with none, even if everything else is equal.

Category selection is the highest-leverage decision

Your primary category is the single most important ranking factor on Google Business Profile. Choosing "Roofing Contractor" vs. "Roofer" vs. "Roofing Service" can shift your rankings across thousands of queries. Most businesses pick based on intuition and leave massive ranking potential on the table. We pick based on data.

COMMON QUESTIONS

GBP management, demystified.

The questions we get most often from business owners exploring professional GBP management.

How long does it take to see results from GBP management?

Most clients see measurable improvement in 30-60 days and meaningful 3-pack movement within 60-90 days. The first wins are usually review volume lift and reply rate — those happen within weeks. Geo-grid ranking improvements come next, then overall call volume and form fill lift. By 90 days, the profile is transformed.

How many reviews should I be getting per month?

Depends on your industry and competitive landscape. As a general benchmark: home services should aim for 8-20 new reviews/month, medical/dental for 10-30, legal for 3-10, and restaurants for 20-50+. Our review acquisition systems typically 3-5× whatever your current rate is within 60 days — without buying reviews or anything sketchy.

What if we have a suspended profile or review problems?

We handle profile suspension appeals, category disputes, and review removal requests (for reviews that violate Google’s policies). Not every negative review can be removed — Google’s bar is high — but fake reviews, competitor attacks, and policy violations can often be handled successfully. We’ve reinstated dozens of suspended profiles in the past two years.

Do you manage multiple locations?

Yes. We regularly manage 5-100+ GBPs under a single brand. Multi-location management uses our bulk workflow tools for posts, reviews, and reporting, with per-location strategy layered on top where needed. Scales cleanly from small chains to national brands.

What's the difference between your service and hiring an in-house manager?

An in-house manager costs $50-85K/year fully loaded for one person who has to also learn SEO, local algorithm changes, citation management, review platforms, and dispute workflows. Our service is a fraction of that cost and draws on a team that’s been specializing in local SEO for 8+ years. If you have complex enterprise needs, we work alongside in-house teams too.

What's your review acquisition approach — is it compliant?

100% compliant with Google’s policies. We use automated SMS and email requests sent to real customers after real transactions, linking directly to your Google review URL. No review gating (where you filter out bad reviews before they post — that’s a TOS violation), no incentivized reviews, no fake review generation. Just a systematic way to ask happy customers to share their experience.

Do you post on Google Posts every week?

Yes — one post per week minimum, often two. Posts are written in your brand voice, rotate through offer/update/event/product formats, and include on-brand imagery. Every post is crafted to either drive clicks to your site, encourage a call, or signal freshness to Google’s algorithm.

How is GBP management different from local SEO?

GBP management focuses specifically on your Google Business Profile and its ranking in the local 3-pack. Local SEO is a broader practice that includes your GBP but also covers local landing pages, citations, on-site local signals, and general organic local ranking. The two compound — managing your GBP while running local landing pages yields much better results than either alone. For businesses where both matter, we usually recommend our integrated approach.
