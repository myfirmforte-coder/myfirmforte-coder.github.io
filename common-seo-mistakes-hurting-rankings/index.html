10 SEO Mistakes Costing You Rankings (Spotted in 100+ Audits)

Technical SEOJune 7, 20265 min readRank Ready Team

Common SEO mistakes are rarely complicated.

After 100 audits, a list emerges. Same 10 mistakes. Same impact. Same fixes. Run through this list this weekend — odds are you’re making at least 3 of them.

What’s surprising isn’t how advanced the problems are.

It’s how often they appear on otherwise well-built websites.

We’ve seen seven-figure companies lose rankings because of broken redirects. We’ve seen local businesses disappear from map results because of incorrect Google Business Profile categories. We’ve seen sites spend thousands on content while ignoring search intent entirely.

The good news?

Most of these SEO errors can be identified and fixed quickly.

Let’s walk through the ten most common ranking killers we continue to find during audits.

Mistake 1: Targeting Keywords Without Checking SERP Intent

This remains one of the most expensive common SEO mistakes.

Businesses often target keywords based on search volume without examining what Google actually ranks.

Intent beats volume

If Google ranks product pages and you’re publishing blog posts, you’re fighting the algorithm.

Before creating content, review the first page.

  • Are results informational?
  • Are they transactional?
  • Are they local?
  • Are they comparison pages?

The page type Google rewards tells you what searchers want.Google’s SEO Starter Guide also emphasizes creating pages that align with user needs rather than simply targeting keywords with high search volume.

Ignore that signal and rankings become significantly harder.

Mistake 2: Using GBP Categories That Don’t Match What You Actually Do

Local businesses frequently sabotage their own visibility.

Wrong categories create relevance problems

Your primary Google Business Profile category should reflect your main service.

We’ve audited businesses using categories that were only loosely related to what they actually offered.

That weakens local relevance and reduces visibility.

Category selection remains one of the strongest local SEO signals available.

For service businesses, fixing category alignment can create measurable improvements surprisingly quickly.

Mistake 3: Title Tags That Match the H1

This mistake wastes valuable search real estate.

Title tags and H1s serve different purposes

Your H1 explains the page topic.

Your title tag helps attract clicks from search results.

They shouldn’t be identical in most situations.

Example:

H1: SEO Audit Services

Title Tag: SEO Audit Services | Find Ranking Issues Fast

The additional context creates stronger click-through opportunities.

It’s a simple fix that appears constantly during audits.

Mistake 4: No FAQ Schema on Pages With FAQ Content

Many websites include FAQ sections but fail to mark them up properly.

Missed structured data opportunities

If FAQ content exists, FAQ schema often should too.

Structured data helps search engines understand page content more clearly.

It’s not a ranking hack.

It’s a clarity signal.

Many sites invest time creating useful FAQs while ignoring the technical layer that supports them.

Mistake 5: 404s on Old URLs Instead of 301 Redirects

Few ranking issues waste authority faster than unnecessary 404 errors.

Link equity disappears

When old pages receive links and then disappear without redirects, valuable authority can be lost.

Every migration, redesign, and content cleanup should include redirect planning.

Use:

  • 301 redirects for permanent changes
  • Redirect maps during migrations
  • Regular crawl audits

Many sites unknowingly lose rankings because historical authority never reaches replacement pages.Comprehensive SEO audits frequently uncover redirect issues that have been quietly suppressing rankings for months or even years.

Mistake 6: Internal Links With “Click Here” Anchor Text

Internal links help search engines understand page relationships.

Generic anchors waste context

“Click here” tells Google almost nothing.

Compare:

  • Click here
  • Technical SEO audit checklist

The second option provides context, relevance, and stronger topical signals.

Anchor text remains one of the easiest SEO best practices to improve.

Mistake 7: No Tracking on Phone Calls From Organic Search

This mistake creates reporting blind spots.

You can’t optimize what you can’t measure

Many local businesses track forms but ignore calls.

That’s a major problem.

For service businesses, phone calls often represent the most valuable conversions.

Without call tracking, you’re making decisions with incomplete data.

Ranking reports don’t pay bills.

Revenue does.

Mistake 8: Service Pages With No Schema

Service pages remain one of the most overlooked opportunities in local SEO.

Help search engines understand your services

Appropriate schema may include:

  • Service Schema
  • LocalBusiness Schema
  • Review Schema
  • FAQ Schema

Structured data improves clarity.

And clarity helps search engines process relevance more efficiently.

Many audits reveal service pages with zero structured data implementation.Missing schema remains one of the most common findings in our analysis of SEO bottlenecks discovered during audits.

Mistake 9: Location Pages With Copy-Paste Content

This is one of the most common local SEO problems.

Changing city names isn’t localization

Many businesses create dozens of location pages using identical content.

The only difference is the city name.

That’s not a local strategy.

It’s duplication.

Strong location pages include:

  • Unique content
  • Local proof
  • Service-specific information
  • Area-relevant examples

Google increasingly rewards pages that provide genuine local value.

Mistake 10: Ignoring AI Engines Entirely

This is the newest entry on our list of common SEO mistakes.

Search behavior is changing

Buyers increasingly use:

  • ChatGPT
  • Perplexity
  • Gemini
  • AI Overviews

If your brand isn’t appearing in AI-generated answers, you’re losing visibility.

We’ve seen companies move from 0% to 74% AI citation rates within 90 days after implementing GEO-focused improvements.

Ignoring AI visibility today feels similar to ignoring mobile optimization a decade ago.

The shift is already happening.

Why Fixing Common SEO Mistakes Delivers Faster Results Than Publishing More Content

Many businesses respond to declining rankings by creating more content.

In many cases, that’s the wrong move.

The biggest gains often come from fixing common SEO mistakes that already exist across the website.

Think about it this way: if search engines are struggling to understand your services, encountering broken redirects, finding duplicate location pages, or missing important schema signals, adding more content simply adds more pages to an already inefficient system.

Optimization usually beats expansion

Across more than 100 audits, we’ve found that resolving foundational SEO errors frequently produces faster ranking improvements than publishing new articles.

Common examples include:

  • Fixing search-intent mismatches
  • Improving internal linking
  • Repairing redirect chains
  • Adding structured data
  • Strengthening local landing pages

These improvements help search engines process and trust existing content more effectively.

The result is often better visibility without creating a single new page.

If you’re experiencing persistent ranking issues, start by auditing what already exists. Most websites have enough content to grow. They simply need fewer common SEO mistakes standing in the way.

How to Scan for All 10 in 60 Minutes

You don’t need a week-long audit to identify most of these issues.

A practical 60-minute review process

  1. Review your top pages for search intent alignment.
  2. Check Google Business Profile categories.
  3. Compare title tags against H1s.
  4. Validate FAQ schema.
  5. Crawl for 404 errors.
  6. Review internal anchor text.
  7. Verify phone-call tracking.
  8. Check service-page schema.
  9. Review location-page uniqueness.
  10. Search for your brand in AI engines.

Most websites don’t have a traffic problem.

They have a handful of fixable issues suppressing visibility.
