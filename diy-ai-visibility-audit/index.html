How to Run an AI Visibility Audit in Under 2 Hours

Technical SEOJune 3, 20267 min readRank Ready Team

AI visibility audit is quickly becoming more valuable than traditional rank tracking.

Knowing whether your brand shows up in ChatGPT’s answers is more valuable than knowing your Google ranking — and you can find out in 2 hours. Here’s the exact protocol.

Most businesses still measure search visibility the way they did five years ago. Rankings. Traffic. Click-through rates.

Those metrics still matter.

But buyers increasingly research vendors through ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews before ever visiting a website.

If your company isn’t appearing in those answers, you’re invisible during a growing part of the buying journey.

The good news: you don’t need enterprise software or a six-week consulting project to understand where you stand.

This AI visibility audit process takes less than two hours and will show you exactly how visible your brand is across today’s major AI platforms.

Why AI Visibility Matters (The Buyer-Research Data)

The traditional search funnel is changing.

Instead of searching Google ten times, opening ten tabs, and comparing websites manually, buyers increasingly ask AI systems direct questions.

Examples include:

  • Best SEO agencies for law firms
  • Top local SEO companies
  • Best CRM for small businesses
  • Most trusted cybersecurity vendors
  • Top GEO agencies

The AI system summarizes the market and recommends brands.

That’s the opportunity.

And that’s the risk.

A business can rank well organically while remaining almost invisible inside AI-generated answers.

We’ve seen companies dominate traditional search and still receive virtually no AI citations.

We’ve also seen brands with modest rankings generate strong AI visibility because they built stronger entity signals.

This is why an AI SEO audit has become a critical addition to traditional SEO measurement.

If buyers are researching through AI, visibility inside AI answers becomes a marketing channel.

Step 1: Build Your Prompt List (15–25 Prompts That Mirror Real Buyers)

The biggest mistake during an AI visibility audit is testing the wrong prompts.

Most marketers use branded searches.

Real buyers rarely do.

Your prompt list should mirror actual research behavior.

Focus on commercial and comparison intent

Build a list containing:

  • Best provider queries
  • Top company queries
  • Comparison queries
  • Alternative queries
  • Recommendation queries

Examples:

  • Best SEO agency for law firms
  • Top GEO agencies in the United States
  • Best local SEO company for dentists
  • Alternatives to large SEO agencies
  • Who are the best AI optimization firms?

Include industry-specific prompts

Generic prompts only tell part of the story.

Add industry-specific searches based on your target audience.

For example:

  • Best SEO agency for home services
  • Best SaaS SEO consultant
  • Top real estate SEO firms
  • Best healthcare SEO companies

A strong AI visibility audit typically includes between fifteen and twenty-five prompts.

That’s enough to identify meaningful patterns without creating unnecessary work.

Step 2: Run Prompts Across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, and Gemini

Once your prompt list is complete, run every prompt through the major AI platforms.

At minimum, include:

  • ChatGPT
  • Perplexity
  • Claude
  • Gemini

Each platform retrieves and evaluates information differently.

Visibility on one platform does not guarantee visibility on another.

Use a consistent process

For every prompt:

  1. Enter the exact prompt
  2. Record results
  3. Capture screenshots
  4. Document citations
  5. Track brand mentions

Create a spreadsheet with:

  • Prompt
  • Platform
  • Brand Mentioned
  • Cited Source
  • Position

Consistency matters.

The objective is identifying visibility patterns rather than relying on isolated examples.

Look beyond rankings

Unlike traditional search, AI systems don’t always provide ranked lists.

Instead, focus on:

  • Presence
  • Frequency
  • Citation quality
  • Context

Being recommended repeatedly matters more than appearing once.

Step 3: Score Each Result — Cited, Mentioned, or Absent

Now it’s time to turn observations into data.

Every prompt should receive one of three scores.

Cited

The strongest outcome.

Your brand appears and is supported by a source reference.

This indicates strong AI visibility.

Mentioned

Your brand appears but receives no citation.

This is still valuable visibility but generally less powerful than a direct citation.

Absent

Your brand does not appear.

This indicates an opportunity gap.

Most businesses discover far more absent results than expected.

Calculate your visibility score

Create a simple formula:

  • Cited = 2 points
  • Mentioned = 1 point
  • Absent = 0 points

Then calculate your score across all prompts.

This creates a benchmark you can compare against competitors later.

A proper AI visibility audit turns subjective observations into measurable performance.

Step 4: Identify Which Sources AI Engines Pull From

This is where the real insights begin.

Most businesses focus on whether they appear.

The smarter question is why they appear.

Document every citation source

Common sources include:

  • Industry publications
  • Review sites
  • Directories
  • Wikipedia
  • Company websites
  • News coverage

Patterns emerge quickly.

If AI systems repeatedly cite the same source, that source likely influences visibility within your industry.

Look for entity signals

Many AI engines rely heavily on entity understanding.

Businesses with stronger entity development frequently outperform competitors.

If you’re missing key entity signals, our guide to Wikipedia and Wikidata entity establishment explains why those assets increasingly influence AI discovery.

Step 5: Compare Your Visibility vs. Three Competitors

An AI visibility audit becomes significantly more valuable when viewed in context.

That’s why competitor analysis matters.

Select three direct competitors and repeat the same process.

Track the same metrics

  • Citations
  • Mentions
  • Visibility score
  • Source frequency

This comparison often reveals opportunities immediately.

Competitors may be receiving citations from sources where your brand has no presence.

They may also appear consistently for prompts where you’re completely absent.

Those gaps become the roadmap.

Step 6: Build the Gap-Fill Action List

Every audit should end with actions.

Not observations.

Prioritize visibility gaps

Common opportunities include:

  • Missing industry mentions
  • Weak entity profiles
  • Insufficient topical coverage
  • Limited brand citations
  • Thin supporting content

Focus on the gaps appearing repeatedly across platforms.

Those usually generate the largest returns.

Our ChatGPT GEO playbook covers many of the tactics used to close those gaps.

Tools That Can Automate This

Manual audits work well.

Software can accelerate the process.

Popular options include:

  • Otterly
  • Profound
  • Peec.ai

These tools track AI mentions, citations, visibility trends, and competitive benchmarks automatically.

They’re particularly useful for larger brands running ongoing monitoring programs.

What Good Results Look Like (The Benchmark)

Most businesses ask the same question:

What’s a good score?

There’s no universal benchmark.

But a useful framework looks like this:

  • 0–20% visibility = weak
  • 20–40% visibility = developing
  • 40–60% visibility = competitive
  • 60%+ visibility = strong

NorthPeak SaaS moved from 0% AI citation visibility to 74% in ninety days after strengthening entity development and semantic coverage.

That’s what strong progress looks like.

The goal isn’t perfection.

The goal is becoming more visible than competitors.

  • Persistent AI visibility gaps
  • Competitive industries

  • Enterprise content libraries
  • Rapid growth goals

At that point, visibility tracking becomes less important than visibility improvement.

Common Mistakes That Distort an AI Visibility Audit

Most businesses don’t struggle because they skip an AI visibility audit.

They struggle because they measure the wrong things.

The most common mistake is using branded prompts exclusively.

If you ask ChatGPT about your company by name, there’s a good chance you’ll appear in the answer.

That doesn’t tell you how visible you are during actual buyer research.

Real buyers ask category-level questions.

They search for recommendations, alternatives, comparisons, and best-of lists.

Those are the prompts that matter.

Other common audit mistakes

  • Testing only one AI platform
  • Using fewer than ten prompts
  • Ignoring citation sources
  • Comparing against the wrong competitors
  • Tracking mentions but not citations

A strong AI visibility audit focuses on patterns rather than individual results.

One mention doesn’t matter much.

Consistent visibility across dozens of prompts does.

How Often Should You Run an AI Visibility Audit?

Unlike traditional rankings, AI visibility can change quickly.

New citations, fresh content, industry mentions, and entity signals can influence recommendations within weeks.

That’s why AI visibility should be monitored regularly.

A practical reporting schedule

  • Monthly for competitive industries
  • Quarterly for most businesses
  • After major content campaigns
  • After digital PR initiatives
  • Following significant website changes

Google’s documentation on AI Overviews highlights how AI-generated search experiences continue evolving, making ongoing monitoring increasingly important.

The objective isn’t checking visibility every day.

The objective is identifying trends before competitors do.

Businesses that monitor AI visibility consistently tend to identify citation opportunities earlier and close visibility gaps faster.

That’s one reason the best-performing brands treat an AI visibility audit as an ongoing process rather than a one-time project.

The New Visibility Layer

A few years ago, understanding search visibility meant checking rankings.

Today, it means understanding whether AI systems recommend your brand when buyers ask questions.

That’s why every marketing team should run an AI visibility audit.

It takes less than two hours.

The insights often influence the next six months of strategy.

The businesses that win AI search won’t necessarily be the ones with the most content.

They’ll be the ones that become the most trusted, cited, and understood entities.
